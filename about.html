About TechHarbour

TechHarbour is a Durban-based IT company dedicated to helping local home users and small businesses stay connected, secure and productive. We specialize in mobile and remote IT support, including computer, mobile and printer repairs, networking, cybersecurity basics, and small-scale website or Python projects. TechHarbour is built around real-world support: mobile visits across Durban, remote troubleshooting, and drop-off by appointment only when needed. Whether you're a home user needing a fast repair, a small business wanting dependable network help, or a local startup looking for practical digital solutions, we provide affordable, grounded support that grows with you.
